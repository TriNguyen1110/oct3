## Food adapter source findings and verified corrections

Reviewed `src/browser/doordash.ts` SHA256 `d58a9db0d41b6b9f4f7bd484c6c76b3a046cba99ce8d5319e2e7911ad244b0d4` and its shared browser wrapper. No source edits or extra provider probes.

1. **Prepared-form evidence is stronger than the checks.** Lines55–67 require a visible store label, click option labels, and see an Add to order $6.60 button. They never read back the selected store or checked modifiers. Lines68–71 nevertheless assert exact store, milk and sweetness selections. A visible price cannot establish those zero-cost choices or current location. Before claiming a prepared form, require final selected-store and checked-value readback; otherwise describe only observed controls/prices. This is a source-level gap; no fabricated live outcome was observed in the latest failed run.
2. **No network-level write prevention in this adapter.** Lines47–64 perform direct remote clicks/fills, while `withSurfskyPage` has no request mutation gate. The adapter deliberately does not activate Add to order, checkout or payment, but those control choices are the current boundary; the component-tool read-only policy does not wrap these direct calls. Wording that checkout “remains locked” by passkey (lines71/74) must not imply this browser is deterministically prevented from merchant writes. Require a scoped network guard before making that guarantee or retain a narrower research-only description. No unauthorized order is alleged or observed.

Both findings were sent to coordinator before this report. Food remains **partial / not verified complete**. No permission for further registration/orders follows from this audit.
