Cue retains/adapts the classic Perlin displacement functions on real icosahedron
geometry. Reimplemented with existing Three.js, no R3F or remote HDRI. Added
polished gold physical material, procedural studio environment, orbital rings,
turquoise satellite and accessible bounded rotation. On-demand rendering only.
